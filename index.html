    About us

    Small charity. Direct impact. Nothing hidden.

    Her International is run by a volunteer board of directors in Kelowna, British Columbia. We believe education is the most powerful way to change a woman's life, and the lives of everyone around her.

    Our directors are unpaid. We work with trusted local partners who know each student and community personally, so your donation goes to girls and women rather than to overhead.

    Your donation goes to three places

    1. 1
      Girls in rural Nepal. Scholarships cover school fees, uniforms, supplies and exam costs, so girls stay in school instead of being pulled out to work or marry early.
    2. 2
      Women in Canada. Newcomer and marginalized women get English practice, mentors and life-skills workshops.
    3. 3
      Keeping the lights on, minimally. Bank fees, accounting, our annual audit and donor receipts. That's it.

    • Girls from low-income households

    Canada

    She may be a newcomer still learning English, or a woman re-entering the workforce. Your support connects her to a mentor, a community and practical skills.

    • Newcomer and immigrant women
    • Women facing barriers to employment
    • Based in the Okanagan region

    Our programs

    Two programs, one mission: empowering women and girls through education

    Nepal Girls' Scholarship Program

    Our longest-running program, active since 2005. We fund the full cost of keeping a girl in school and work with local partners to check on attendance and progress.

    What it covers
    Tuition and school fees · uniforms and shoes · backpacks and supplies · textbooks · exam registration

    Canadian Women's Empowerment

    Mentorship, language and life-skills programs for marginalized women in Canada, run by volunteers in the Kelowna area.

    What it covers
    English conversation circles · one-to-one mentorship · workshops on budgeting, résumés and interviews

      Questions donors ask

      Frequently asked

      Will I get a tax receipt?

      Yes. Her International is a registered Canadian charity, and CanadaHelps emails an official receipt automatically.

      How do you choose which girls get scholarships?

      Our partners in Nepal identify girls who are at risk of leaving school for financial reasons. They follow each student's attendance and progress through the year.

      Can I choose which program my donation supports?

      Yes. Leave a note with your donation saying "Nepal" or "Canada" and we'll put it toward that program. Undesignated gifts go wherever the need is greatest.

      Is a monthly donation better than a one-time gift?

      Both help. Monthly giving lets us promise a girl her scholarship for the whole school year, so it's especially valuable to us.
